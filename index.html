Rules

Defending corporation size
Corporations with fewer than 10 members are war immune. A corporation must have at least 10 members to be declared war upon.
10 members
The base war cost starts at 1,000,000,000 ISK per week.
10–20 members
The cost drops rapidly from 1,000,000,000 ISK to 500,000,000 ISK. Each additional member reduces the base cost by 50,000,000 ISK.
Base Cost = 1,000,000,000 − (Members − 10) × 50,000,000
20–500 members
The base cost gradually decreases from 500,000,000 ISK at 20 members to 0 ISK at 500 members.
Base Cost = 500,000,000 × (500 − Members) ÷ 480
500 members
War cost from the defender-size calculation becomes free.
Attacker active wars
The multiplier increases linearly from 1.00× at one active war to 2.00× at ten active wars. Ten or more active wars remain at 2.00× with no additional increase.
1 war = 1.00×
2 wars = 1.11×
3 wars = 1.22×
4 wars = 1.33×
5 wars = 1.44×
6 wars = 1.56×
7 wars = 1.67×
8 wars = 1.78×
9 wars = 1.89×
10+ wars = 2.00×
Weekly bill
The weekly war bill is the defending corporation's base cost multiplied by the attacker's active-war multiplier.
Weekly Bill = Base Cost × Active War Multiplier
War duration
A war lasts for a maximum of one week and does not automatically extend. If no surrender is accepted, the war ends when the one-week period expires.
Surrender
Surrender is available only during the first week. The CEOs must meet in a station. The defending CEO must press Accept on the attacking CEO's surrender terms. If the terms are not accepted, the war continues until the one-week expiration.
