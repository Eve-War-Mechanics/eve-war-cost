Rules

Defending corporation: 1–9 members are war immune. The calculator begins at 10 members.
10 members: 1,000,000,000 ISK base war cost per week.
10–20 members: Cost decreases by 50,000,000 ISK per additional member, reaching 500,000,000 ISK at 20.
20–500 members: Cost decreases gradually to 0 ISK at 500 members.
Active wars: 1 war = 1.00×. The multiplier increases linearly to 2.00× at 10 wars. 10+ wars remain capped at 2.00×.
Weekly bill: Defender base cost × active-war multiplier.
War duration: Maximum 4 weeks. There is no automatic extension beyond 4 weeks.
Automated surrender: Available through the in-game system for the entire time the war is active. The system processes the surrender. Using surrender ends the war and starts a 4-week mandatory peace period.
War expiration: If the war reaches the 4-week maximum, it ends automatically and starts a 4-week mandatory peace period.
Direct CEO agreement: CEOs may meet in a station to bypass the automated system menus. If both CEOs agree in person, they may end the war directly. No peace period applies.
